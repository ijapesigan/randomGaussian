Let $\boldsymbol{X}$ be a $k$-dimensional random vector

$$
\boldsymbol{X} = 
\left(
\begin{array}{c}
X_{1} \\
X_{2} \\
\vdots \\
X_{k} \\
\end{array}
\right)
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{X}
\sim 
\mathcal{N}_{k}
\left(
\boldsymbol{\mu},
\boldsymbol{\Sigma}
\right)
$$

where

$$
\boldsymbol{\mu} \in \mathbb{R}^{k},
\boldsymbol{\Sigma} \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times k}
$$

The affine transformation of $\boldsymbol{X}$ given by $\boldsymbol{Y}$

$$
\boldsymbol{Y}  =
\boldsymbol{A}
\boldsymbol{X}
+
\boldsymbol{v}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{Y}
\sim 
\mathcal{N}_{k}
\left(
\boldsymbol{A}
\boldsymbol{\mu}
+
\boldsymbol{v},
\boldsymbol{A}
\boldsymbol{\Sigma}
\boldsymbol{A}^{\prime}
\right)
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{Z}
\sim 
\mathcal{N}_{k}
\left(
\boldsymbol{0},
\boldsymbol{I}
\right)
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{X}  =
\boldsymbol{A}
\boldsymbol{Z}
+
\boldsymbol{v}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{X}
\sim 
\mathcal{N}_{k}
\left(
\boldsymbol{A}
\boldsymbol{0}
+
\boldsymbol{v},
\boldsymbol{A}
\boldsymbol{I}
\boldsymbol{A}^{\prime}
\right)
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{X}
\sim 
\mathcal{N}_{k}
\left(
\boldsymbol{v},
\boldsymbol{A}
\boldsymbol{A}^{\prime}
\right)
$$

$$
\boldsymbol{\mu} = 
\boldsymbol{v},
\boldsymbol{\Sigma} =
\boldsymbol{A}
\boldsymbol{A}^{\prime}
$$
